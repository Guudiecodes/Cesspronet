Privacy

Last updated November 2026. This explains what Cesspro Technologies keeps when you use CessproNet WiFi.

What we keep

  • Your phone number, to reach you about your service and to find your order.
  • Your device address, so your plan can switch on for your device.
  • Your order and payment reference.

Payments

Card payments are handled by our payment provider. We do not store your card details.

How we use it

Only to run the service, give support and keep the network safe. We do not sell your information.
