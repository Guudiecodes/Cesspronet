Help and support

Most problems take a minute to fix. Start here, and call us if you are still stuck on 09035703720.

How do I get online?

Connect to the CessproNet WiFi, open the page that appears, tap Buy a plan, choose a plan and pay. You are connected automatically when the payment is confirmed.

I paid but I am not online

  1. Keep the page open for a minute. Activation can take up to a minute.
  2. Open Check my order and enter your order number.
  3. Still stuck? Call us with your order number and phone number.

I have a code

Open the sign-in page and tap I have a code. Enter the code and your phone number, then tap Activate code.

The sign-in page does not open

  1. Make sure you are connected to the CessproNet WiFi.
  2. Turn mobile data off for a moment.
  3. Open any website that starts with http, for example neverssl.com.

Which payment methods can I use?

You can pay online (card or bank transfer, depending on what is switched on), or ask for bank account details by SMS for a small fee.

Can I use my plan on more than one device?

That depends on the plan. Each plan shows how many devices it covers.
